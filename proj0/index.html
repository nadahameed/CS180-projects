Becoming Friends with Your Camera

Part 1: Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

Taking the photo from up close causes the face's proportions to look distorted. My phone camera uses an ultra wide lens, which has a wider field of view. So in the zoomed photo, the nose and central areas of the face are larger, while the cheeks and chin are pulled away. However, taking the photo from a distance reduces these distortions, as the differences in the facial features' distances become relatively smaller.

Part 2: Architectural Perspective Compression

This is the opposite of the previous part. At first, we start farther away and zoom in. Again, the distance between the camera and the building features become negligible with respect to each other, so the house looks flatter. Up close though, the distances in depth are more pronounced, which is why different features look smaller or larger compared to each other.
